About Araku Valley

About Araku

Araku Valley is a beautiful hill station located in the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh. It is famous for its green mountains, coffee plantations, waterfalls and tribal traditions.

The peaceful environment and scenic landscapes make Araku a wonderful destination for nature lovers, photographers and tourists.

Why Visit Araku?

  • Beautiful mountain landscapes
  • Famous coffee plantations
  • Waterfalls and caves
  • Rich tribal culture
  • Peaceful natural environment
